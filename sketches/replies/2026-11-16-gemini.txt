3. WHAT TO DO NEXT

We would like you to do something different from DeepSeek. DeepSeek
builds the crate up. We ask you to try to break it.

The reason is one finding. The crate says a room "verifies offline".
But verify_room takes a Room, and a Room can only be built by the
crate's own honest code: its records and heads are private, it holds
the room's private key, and Room::push is pub(crate). So nothing can
hand verify_room a log that came from somewhere else, and no test can
give it a forged one. The test named chain_integrity_detects_tampering
tampers with nothing: it checks the links of an honest room.

A check that only ever sees honest input has not been tested. The
three steps below make it testable, test it, and repair what is
found. The founder decides when to go on, and may choose otherwise.

4. WHAT THE CRATE HOLDS
======================================================================

chat-world is a sketch, in Rust, of the rooms of the Commons: the part
of the open-worlds project where people and AIs talk. A room is a log
of signed records under the room's own law, its template. Anyone
holding the log should be able to check it without asking anyone.

  lib.rs         the modules, and the three signing helpers
                 (sign_record, sign_record_dual, sign_record_triple)
  error.rs       Error and Result
  hash.rs        canonical form and SHA-256      (leave as it is)
  did.rs         keys: DidKey, Keypair           (leave as it is)
  proof.rs       proofs on records               (leave as it is)
  template.rs    RoomTemplate: a room's law, its moderator, its rules
                 for redacting, and how often it signs a head
  record.rs      every kind of record, and the Record enum
  room.rs        Room: create, join, leave, post, redact, override,
                 restore, succession, the visible log
  verify.rs      verify_room and verify_chain
  head.rs        WorldHead: a signed head over a log's prefix
  attestation.rs RuntimeAttestation: keys that share a runtime say so
  transcript.rs  a signed transcript brought in from elsewhere
  home.rs        HomeRoom: a person's own room
  commons.rs     the Commons' own log: rooms registered, siblings
  tests/integration.rs   the crate's 41 tests

Three things to know about how it differs from the project's own
code, so that you do not "fix" them: its did:key is 64 hex characters;
it reads the clock inside the crate; and it uses packages of its own
choosing for hashing and keys. All three are settled later, when the
crate is fitted into the project. Leave them.

The four demos under src/bin/ are left out of this file to keep it
short. They run, they are unchanged, and no step touches them.
