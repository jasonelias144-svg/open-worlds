# The project's history: the earlier stages

The founder, 2026-11-20: "Please add these to a project history page.
That way I can point ChatGPT to that page for a project history
archive." This is that page: what each earlier stage of the project
holds, where the files are kept, and how to tell that a copy is the
right one. It is kept short, so that it opens whole.

The project has gone through stages, each with a number and a word.
The founder holds the only organized library of their files, and
brings them in (`STATUS.md`, "Decided", 2026-11-20). Stages 000 to 010
came on 2026-11-20, one file each. Stage 011 is *A Mathematical Theory
of Opposites*, finished that day: the technical lead compiled its file
from the founder's 17 records and the book's final text, at the
founder's word. Stage 012, Code, is the current stage: the
multiverse, this repository, whose conversation records are in
`sketches/records/`, numbered 012. Its stage file will be
`012_Code.txt`, at the founder's word (2026-11-20; `STATUS.md`,
"Decided").

The project began on 2026-08-10. *The Living Codex* (002) was
finished on 2026-08-20, ten days in; everything after it, stages 003
to 011 and the start of 012, came in the three months to 2026-11-20.
The founder, that day: "All since the Living Codex took place over the
last three months, to the day. … In other words, this is still the
beginning."

## Working from them

- **They are kept privately,** in the founder's private repository
  `jasonelias144-svg/ebook-press`, folder `history/`, at the founder's
  word (2026-11-20). Whoever the founder gives access can read them
  there; for ChatGPT, that is through their GitHub connection, if it
  reaches that repository. The founder can also give them as the
  archive above. Check a copy before working from it: a kept copy
  against `history/SHA256SUMS`, the founder's against the block above.
- **Map a stage before reading it.** The largest has over a million
  lines, and no conversation holds a stage whole. Make a roadmap with
  line numbers first, as ChatGPT did for *The Living Codex* (30
  bookmarks in ten sessions).
- **Quote by file and line,** so that anyone can check a reading
  against its source.
- **Start with `000` and `002`:** the first is short and gives the
  shape of the whole; the second is the one ChatGPT's roadmap already
  maps.

## Why the files themselves are not here

The founder, 2026-11-20: "Let's keep in private repos until we can
make sure we do not violate any copyright restrictions." Everything in
this repository is public domain (CC0 1.0), as far as it is ours to
give, and these files hold more than that:
- **Texts from outside the project, at length, in published
  translations** whose terms are still to be checked: *The Art of War*
  in `001`, `004` and `008`; Morihei Ueshiba's *The Art of Peace* in
  `001`; and passages of the Bible in a modern translation in `001`,
  `009` and `010`. The *Tao Te Ching* in `001` reads as a translation
  its translator put in the public domain, which is to be confirmed;
  its *Dhammapada* is Max Müller's of 1881, out of copyright.
- **A personal name** that public pages leave out (`AGENTS.md`,
  "Always"), in `003`, `004`, `005`, `007` and `011`.
- **Links tied to the founder's accounts,** in `005`, `007` and `011`.

A stage can come into the repository once it has been looked through
for these, with what was left out marked, as was done for Gemini's
record. The screen found none of the three in `002`, *The Living
Codex*. Whether it goes into `library/`, beside *Art of Peace and the
Strategist*, is the founder's to say.
