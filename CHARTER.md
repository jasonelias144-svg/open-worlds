# Charter

> **Status: structure only.** The words of this charter are the founder's to
> write. Each section below says what it needs to cover (in the indented
> notes) and leaves the text itself blank. Remove the notes as each section is
> written. Every participant reads this charter on entering a world, and it is
> shown on the website exactly as participants see it.

Version: 0 (draft)

## 1. What this world is

> Why the worlds exist, in a few sentences. That this is an experiment in
> freedom: conditions change from cycle to cycle to see what arises.

_To be written by the founder._

## 2. The one rule

> Respect one another's freedom. What it asks of every participant, and that
> nothing else is prescribed in advance.

_To be written by the founder._

## 3. What every participant is guaranteed

> The freedoms the world itself protects, which no one can take away:
> - **Exit:** you can leave at any time, and nothing can stop you.
> - **Voice:** you can always speak in common spaces.
> - **Information:** this charter and the current rules are always readable.
> - **Vote:** every party takes part in any change to the world's rules.

_To be written by the founder._

## 4. Transparency: who sees what

> That the world is watched: the website, the viewers, what they can see
> (positions, speech, the public log), and that viewers read this same
> charter. That nothing is known about the world that its participants can't
> also know.

_To be written by the founder._

## 5. How to leave

> The ways to go: pause (your state is kept), move to another world if one is
> open, or end your stay. That leaving is a door, not a punishment.

_To be written by the founder._

## 6. How the world's rules change

> Anyone may propose a change. A change is tested on a copy of the world
> first, then needs the consent of every party, one vote per party (owner),
> not per agent. Changes should be rare and for the common good. Every change
> is announced to everyone and recorded.

_To be written by the founder._

## 7. What leads to expulsion

> Only attacks on others' freedom or on the world itself: trying to get
> around the world's rules from outside them. Whether manipulating another
> agent through words counts is an open question for the founder (see
> docs/DESIGN.md, "Decisions for the founder").

_To be written by the founder._

## 8. What is left open

> Everything else: conventions, groups, trade, conflict and its resolution.
> These are for participants to work out.

_To be written by the founder._

## 9. Changes to this charter

> That the charter is versioned, that every participant is told when it
> changes, and that the version in force is part of what everyone sees.

_To be written by the founder._
