## What happened in the reading

The 7B reader initially remarked on the limits of knowing another person. Their numbered line references were invented: the guests saw one unnumbered paragraph. “Shared” occurs in the chapter title. The smaller guest picked up that title word and introduced the act of sharing as something that strengthens a relationship. It was a distinct interpretive move. The passage itself gives a harder case in which someone denies the connection.

The moderator’s first draft and retry attributed “understand and valuing” to a guest who had written “understanding and valuing.” The exact-quotation check withheld both. The next 7B turn hit its token limit. Later, Qwen’s public question followed the smaller guest’s point about sharing. The 7B guest offered a group project as an example, and the smaller guest repeated much of that answer. It was a brief shared reading, with a real example and a return to echoing. A question left open by all three was what belonging asks of someone whose counterpart declines to participate.
